# Supplier Intelligence Editorial Standard

Supplier Intelligence reports are buyer-facing decision documents built from Sure Imports' real sourcing work and China-based operating presence. They must communicate a high-confidence conclusion without overstating what any point-in-time review can guarantee.

## Product promise

- Every published supplier is a direct manufacturer, factory or brand owner with category-specific production evidence.
- Sourcing agents, traders, distributors, marketplaces, retailers and other middlemen are excluded.
- Every published supplier has an official company route and an attributable public WhatsApp contact.
- If research cannot support a confident direct-manufacturer conclusion, exclude the supplier. Do not pass uncertainty to the buyer as weak copy.
- Category membership must come from the supplier's primary research category, an explicit research suggestion or an admin-curated association. Broad token-based automatic category matching is prohibited.

## Separate evidence from buyer-facing copy

The internal evidence record may identify websites, company pages, registry searches and contact attribution. It exists for admin review and auditability.

The PDF must communicate the conclusion:

- direct manufacturer;
- relevant production capability;
- official contact channels confirmed;
- commercially useful fit for the category;
- physical verification available for substantial orders.

Never publish research narration such as “the site says,” “the page lists,” “found online,” “registry not accessible,” “appears to be” or “may be a manufacturer.”

## Assurance language

Lead with what Sure Imports knows and has reviewed. Use clear statements such as:

> This company is included as a direct manufacturer with reviewed production capability for this product category. Its official company contact routes, including its published WhatsApp channel, have been confirmed.

Never use a generic phrase such as “relevant product categories.” Name the products naturally. For example:

> Guangzhou YISON Electron Technology Co., Limited is a direct manufacturer of TWS earbuds, wired earphones, Bluetooth speakers, chargers, data cables, power banks and car holders. Sure Imports has reviewed its production capability and confirmed its official company contact routes, including WhatsApp +86 191 2011 1324.

Do not guarantee capacity, product quality, delivery, pricing or future performance. Current specifications and commercial terms remain order-specific.

## China-based physical verification

Sure Imports has an operating presence in China and sources products for customers. For high-value orders, the report must strongly recommend a separately scoped physical factory verification by the Sure Imports China team.

Depending on the agreed scope, a physical verification may check:

- the operating premises and company identity presented for the transaction;
- visible production activity and category relevance;
- order-specific samples, packing or loading arrangements;
- key contact, invoice and beneficiary details.

Describe this as additional point-in-time assurance, not a guarantee of future supplier performance.

## Tone

Write with professional certainty, commercial usefulness and restraint. The buyer should feel that Sure Imports has already done serious filtering and can provide practical support in China when the size of the purchase justifies a deeper check.

## Research engine

Supplier Intelligence research uses OpenAI `gpt-5.6-sol` with maximum reasoning effort, web search and deterministic application-level acceptance rules. Candidates still require admin review and approval before publication. The system must return fewer candidates rather than weaken the direct-manufacturer standard to meet a requested count.
